How the year runs

  • Classes — 19 Sunday teaching sessions across the six units.
  • Presentation — students present what they have learned to the group.
  • Seva — a service project closes each unit, putting the theme into action.
  • Unit 6 — the Easwaramma Day programme: casting, then practices on Saturdays and Sundays through April, a stage rehearsal on Friday May 7, and the programme itself on Sunday May 9.

Parents also take part as partners during each unit. Those dates are arranged within the class, so your child’s teacher will confirm them — as they will any change to the dates above.
