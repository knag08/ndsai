How a unit works

  • Classes 1–3 — the teaching sessions for the unit theme.
  • Parent participation — parents join the class as partners, once every unit.
  • Presentation — students present what they have learned to the group.
  • Seva — a service project closes the unit, putting the theme into action.

The Bal Vikas calendar follows the PISD school calendar. Dates can shift; your child’s teacher will confirm any change.
