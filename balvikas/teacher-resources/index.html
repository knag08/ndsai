Teacher resources

Curated listening from the Sri Sathya Sai Media Centre, gathered for our Bal Vikas teachers.

With humble pranams at Swami’s Lotus Feet, this is a list of external resources from the Sri Sathya Sai Media Centre website that have been curated for use by NDSai Bal Vikas teachers for inspiration. There are snippets in many of the items that may be relevant across the entire Bal Vikas programme.

This is primarily intended for teachers’ use. If audio is used in class or during parent participation sessions, teachers are encouraged to note down timestamps and play only specific snippets.
