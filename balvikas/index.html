Bal Vikas

Spiritual education for children

Our programme for children is based on the teachings of Sri Sathya Sai Baba, and is dedicated to making sure that children start their journey with God.

Core values

The programme is designed to impart values and principles that align with Swami’s teachings, helping children grow spiritually and ethically. Three ideas hold it together.

Daiva Preeti
Love for God
Papa Bheeti
Fear of wrong-doing
Sangha Neeti
Morality in society

What we aim for

Objectives

General

  • To help the child discern divine providence behind every event.
  • To inculcate an integrated personality and to enable them to incorporate into this the five human values of Truth, Right Action, Peace, Love, and Non-Violence.

Specific

  • To inculcate respect for parents and elders.
  • To promote obedience at home and outside.
  • To instill faith and devotion in God through good devotional practices.
  • To sow the seeds for love and honesty.
  • To enable students to practice kindness, compassion, and tolerance at home and outside.
  • To awaken intuition.
  • To awaken awareness of one's own consciousness.
  • To inculcate self-reliance and leadership.

Practicalities

  • Group 1KG – 3rd Grade
  • Group 24th – 7th Grade
  • Group 38th – 10th Grade

The Bal Vikas calendar follows the PISD school calendar.See all class dates

Admission

  • Parents need to attend an orientation meeting to understand the programme and their role in their child’s moral & spiritual growth.
  • Parent commitment includes the need to participate in “parents-as-partners” once every unit.
  • Every unit includes a service project as part of the programme, and we welcome parents to help conduct them.

Admissions are currently closed. All classes are full for this academic year — please write to us next year.

Our teachers

A group of dedicated volunteers serve as our teachers. They are devotees who love children and are committed to their emotional, moral and spiritual well-being.
