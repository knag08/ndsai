# Audio for the centenary pages

Drop an instrumental here and point `presentationMusic.src` in
`src/data/timeline.ts` at it, for example `/audio/instrumental.mp3`.

It is played only behind the presentation view (`/centenary/timeline/`, the
Present button) and never starts on its own — a guest who scans the QR code
gets silence. The player sits outside the card deck and is never re-rendered,
so the music runs straight through as the cards change, and loops at the end.

**Only add a recording the centre is entitled to publish.** Putting a file here
publishes it on ndsai.org, and a film melody stays in copyright however it is
arranged — an instrumental cover is a derivative of the composition, not a way
around it. A recording made by our own students, or one the rights holder has
licensed, is the way to do this.
