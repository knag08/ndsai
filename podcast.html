Centenary Birthday celebrations of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba

The year 2025 marks a divine opportunity to live His message with renewed inspiration. Swami reminded us that children are the torchbearers of truth and transformation, and when their hearts are shaped by values, society itself becomes stronger.

To honor this sacred occasion, we are launching this Podcast Series, created, hosted, and voiced entirely by our Bal Vikas students. Each episode will explore one of Baba’s profound teachings, connect it to your daily life, and inspire families to practice these values together. Please enjoy, listen, forward, get involved, and motivate your students and friends to implement these practices. Om Sairam.
