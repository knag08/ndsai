About us

A forum to learn, and a place to practise

North Dallas Sathya Sai BalVikas is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation. Learning, devotion and service are promoted here through activities open to children, parents and devotees alike.

Who we are

The center provides a forum to learn and practise the teachings of Sri Sathya Sai Baba. Learning, devotion, as well as service, are promoted through various activities — Sunday morning classes for children, weekly devotional singing, Veda chanting, and a rhythm of service projects that runs through the whole year.

We meet at the Alpha Montessori House in Frisco. Bhajans are held from 11:20 am to 12 noon on Bal Vikas class days, and every student, parent and visitor is warmly encouraged to take part.

Our purpose

  1. Spiritual inquiry, reflection, and practice of teachings.
  2. Spiritual education of children.
  3. Service to community.

The foundation

Five human values

Every part of the programme — every story told, every song sung, every project undertaken — is an attempt to draw out one of these five.

  • Sathya

    Truth

  • Dharma

    Right Conduct

  • Shanti

    Peace

  • Prema

    Love

  • Ahimsa

    Non-violence

Daiva Preeti

Love for God

Papa Bheeti

Fear of wrong-doing

Sangha Neeti

Morality in society

For members

The Nine Point Code of Conduct

The discipline undertaken by members of the Sri Sathya Sai Organization — nine practices that turn belief into a daily habit.

  1. 1

    Daily meditation and prayer.

  2. 2

    Devotional singing/prayer with family members once per week.

  3. 3

    Participation in Sai Spiritual Education by children of the family.

  4. 4

    Participation in community service and other programs of the Organization.

  5. 5

    Regular attendance at devotional meetings conducted by the Organization.

  6. 6

    Regular study of Sathya Sai Baba literature.

  7. 7

    Use of soft, loving speech with everyone.

  8. 8

    Avoidance of talking ill of others, especially in their absence.

  9. 9

    The practice of the principles of Ceiling on Desires, consciously and continuously striving to eliminate the tendency to waste time, money, food, and energy, and utilizing any savings thereby generated for the service of mankind.

Would you like to take part?

There is room in almost every seva we run, and bhajans are open to all. Write to us and we will point you to the right person.
