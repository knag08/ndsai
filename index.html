How we serve

Bal Vikas

Children start the journey with God

Our spiritual education programme for children is based on the teachings of Sri Sathya Sai Baba. Classes take place every Sunday morning at the Alpha Montessori House in Frisco, and run right through the school year alongside unit presentations and service projects.

A group of dedicated volunteers serve as our teachers — devotees who love children and are committed to their emotional, moral and spiritual well-being.

Seva

Service, not charity

Narayan Seva means serving God in the form of man. It is a spiritual practice, and the best way we know to teach our children by example.

Narayana Seva

A home-cooked vegetarian meal served at the Austin Street shelter in Dallas. With Swami’s grace, we have done this since 1985.

For members

The Nine Point Code of Conduct

The daily discipline that members of the Sri Sathya Sai Organization undertake.

  1. 1

    Daily meditation and prayer.

  2. 2

    Devotional singing/prayer with family members once per week.

  3. 3

    Participation in Sai Spiritual Education by children of the family.

  4. 4

    Participation in community service and other programs of the Organization.

  5. 5

    Regular attendance at devotional meetings conducted by the Organization.

  6. 6

    Regular study of Sathya Sai Baba literature.

  7. 7

    Use of soft, loving speech with everyone.

  8. 8

    Avoidance of talking ill of others, especially in their absence.

  9. 9

    The practice of the principles of Ceiling on Desires, consciously and continuously striving to eliminate the tendency to waste time, money, food, and energy, and utilizing any savings thereby generated for the service of mankind.
