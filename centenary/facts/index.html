Do you know?

A hundred things that were built, dug, laid and given away.

1

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba was born in Puttaparthi on 23 November 1926, an auspicious Monday in the lunar month of Kartika, when Lord Shiva is especially adored.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

2

He was born in the Hindu year of Akshaya, a word that means the Never-declining, the Ever-full.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

3

In the early 1900s Puttaparthi was a small hamlet of just two lanes meeting at right angles, not far from the Chitravathi river.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

4

As a schoolboy in Kamalapuram, Sathya woke to the bell of a steam engine at four o'clock every morning and carried two pots of water before going to school.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

5

On 23 May 1940, Sathya called the household around Him and gave sugar candy and flowers out of nowhere; the neighbours rushed in to see.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

6

On 20 October 1940, standing in his brother's doorway, Sathya cast aside his schoolbooks and said, “I am no longer your Sathya.”

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

7

On 25 May 1947, at the age of twenty-one, He set out the mission and purpose of His advent in a letter to His brother, Seshama Raju.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

8

Baba's first mandir at Puttaparthi was inaugurated on Vaikunta Ekadashi, 14 December 1945.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

9

As the number of devotees grew, a mandir was built in February 1947, where He began to live and give darshan.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

10

Prasanthi Nilayam, the Abode of Supreme Peace, was inaugurated on 23 November 1950, Baba's twenty-fifth birthday.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

11

Baba wrote fifteen series of articles for Sanathana Sarathi, among them Prema Vahini.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

12

On His 83rd and 85th birthdays, in November 2008 and 2010, Baba rode on a golden chariot at His devotees' request.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

13

Baba's last discourse was given at the 29th Convocation of the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

14

Baba discarded His bodily vesture on 24 April 2011, after eighty-six years.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

15

The Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations, begun in the 1960s, grew to more than 126 countries, with Prasanthi Nilayam at their centre.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

16

The 85th year of Baba's advent was celebrated across India and in 125 countries.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

17

Puttaparthi lies 125 kilometres north-north-east of Bangalore.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

18

The day at Prasanthi Nilayam begins with the chanting of 21 Aumkars.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

19

Sai Kulwant Hall was built to hold up to 20,000 devotees at a time for Baba's daily darshan.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

20

The spectators' gallery of the Hill View Stadium seats about 30,000 people.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

21

A statue of Hanuman 65 feet high stands at the peak of the hill in Hill View Stadium, built to mark Baba's 65th birthday.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

22

The Meditation Tree is a banyan Baba planted on 29 June 1959, and beneath it He installed a copper Yantra about 15 inches by 10 inches.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

23

That Yantra was materialised out of the sands of the Chitravathi river in April 1959.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

24

The Sarva Dharma Stupa, which stands for the unity of all the world's religions, was inaugurated at Baba's 50th birthday celebrations.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

25

The Chaitanya Jyothi Museum was inaugurated on 18 November 2000, to mark the 75th year of the advent.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

26

An Olympic-standard indoor stadium measuring 100 metres by 60 metres, on a 4.8-acre site, was inaugurated on 22 November 2006 by the President of India.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

27

On 30 December 1997 the Sri Sathya Sai Unity Cup was played at Puttaparthi, with an India XI captained by Sachin Tendulkar.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

28

The Annual Sports and Cultural Meet of Baba's schools and colleges is held on 11 January every year.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

29

Baba inaugurated the Dattatreya temple at Prasanthi Nilayam on 13 January 1986, in answer to the prayers of devotees of Lord Dattatreya.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

30

Radio Sai Global Harmony was launched from Prasanthi Nilayam to carry Baba's message around the world 24 hours a day.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

31

More than 6,000 young people from 85 countries attended the Sai World Youth Conference at Prasanthi Nilayam.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

32

Eleven multipurpose community halls, and a number of temples, have been built in Puttaparthi and the villages around it and handed over to the local bodies.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

33

Anantapur's three rivers run dry for most of the year, and even the groundwater was brackish and so high in fluoride that it was causing skeletal and dental deformities.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

34

In March 1995 the Trust began work to bring pure drinking water to the villages of drought-ravaged Anantapur district.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

35

In November 1995 Baba said that Rayalaseema, today a Raallaseema or rocky region, must be transformed into Ratnalaseema, a land that glitters as a diamond.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

36

The Anantapur drinking water project was completed in a record time of eighteen months.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

37

A total of 1.25 million people were benefited by the Anantapur drinking water project.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

38

About 2,000 kilometres of pipeline of varying diameters were laid for the Anantapur project.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

39

Forty-three sumps, holding between 1 lakh and 25 lakh litres, were built for the Anantapur project.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

40

Eighteen balancing reservoirs, of 3 lakh to 10 lakh litres, were built on the tops of hillocks.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

41

More than 1,500 precast concrete cisterns of 2,500 litres each were installed in the villages.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

42

The first phase was inaugurated on 18 November 1995 by the then Prime Minister of India, Shri P. V. Narasimha Rao.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

43

The completed Anantapur project was handed over to the Government of Andhra Pradesh in October 1997.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

44

India's Ninth Five Year Plan called the project “an unparallel example of private initiative”, carried out without any budgetary support from the State.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

45

On 23 November 1999 the Department of Posts released a postage stamp and a postal cover in recognition of the water project.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

46

Before the second phase, borewells around Puttaparthi had to be drilled deeper than 600 feet, and fluoride ran at 2 to 4 PPM against a safe level of about 1 PPM.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

47

The second phase, begun in 2013, laid 411 kilometres of pipeline at a cost of Rs. 80 crore, for 118 habitations and a population of 2.5 lakhs.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

48

The Anantapur model was repeated to bring water to 320 villages in Medak and Mahabubnagar districts.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

49

That project cost Rs. 530 million, covered 640 square kilometres and served about a million people.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

50

Until December 2004 Chennai needed 750 million litres of water a day and was making do with a mere 250 million.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

51

Of the 15 TMC of Krishna water promised to the city, only half a TMC was actually reaching the Poondi reservoir.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

52

On 19 January 2002 Baba announced that He had determined to bring Krishna water to Chennai.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

53

The capacity of the Kandaleru reservoir was raised more than fourfold, from 16 TMC to 68 TMC.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

54

The Trust lined about 65 kilometres of the most vulnerable stretches of the 150-kilometre canal with concrete.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

55

About 4,000 workers laboured round the clock on the Chennai water project.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

56

The gates of the Kandaleru reservoir were opened on 23 November 2004, Baba's 79th birthday.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

57

The water reached the Andhra Pradesh-Tamil Nadu border in four days, against the 8 to 10 days it used to take.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

58

On 27 November 2004 the Poondi reservoir began to fill with the water devotees call Sai Ganga.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

59

In January 2007 the Chennai Citizens' Conclave packed the Nehru Indoor Stadium to thank Baba for the gift of water to the city.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

60

The Trust took up a project to supply safe drinking water to about 452 upland and tribal habitations in the Godavari districts.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

61

About 212 habitations, with more than 2.20 lakh people, were served in East Godavari district.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

62

About 240 habitations, with more than 4.70 lakh people, were served in West Godavari district.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

63

Around 535 kilometres of pipeline were laid in East Godavari, and around 640 kilometres in West Godavari.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

64

Both Godavari projects were completed and handed to the Government of Andhra Pradesh on 15 September 2007.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

65

The Sri Sathya Sai General Hospital at Prasanthi Nilayam, started in 1956, was the first institution Baba established.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

66

When it opened in 1956 it was the very first medical facility in the entire region.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

67

Baba inaugurated the present General Hospital building on 28 August 1976.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

68

The General Hospital now treats 300 to 400 patients a day, with 80 beds and three operation theatres.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

69

The super-speciality hospital at Prasanthigram is a 230-bed hospital giving tertiary medical care completely free of charge to all.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

70

It was inaugurated on 22 November 1991 by the then Prime Minister of India, on the eve of Baba's 66th birthday.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

71

Its dome rises 90 feet above the hospital.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

72

The Whitefield hospital in Bengaluru is a 333-bed tertiary care hospital, inaugurated by the then Prime Minister of India, Shri Atal Behari Vajpayee.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

73

The foundation stone at Whitefield was laid on 2 September 1999, and the hospital was inaugurated on 19 January 2001.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

74

The Whitefield hospital was built in nine months, a project of six million man hours.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

75

The Whitefield hospital runs twelve modular operation theatres, three intensive care units and three catheterisation labs, round the clock.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

76

The chandelier under the central dome at Whitefield is made of 11,000 individual pieces of glass, every one blown by mouth.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

77

Almost thirty people worked three months to make that chandelier, and five engineers installed it in five days.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

78

Tertiary medical care has been given free of charge at the Whitefield hospital since 2001.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences

79

The Sri Sathya Sai Mobile Hospital, a rural outreach service, was launched by Baba on 30 March 2006.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

80

The Mobile Hospital runs from the 1st to the 12th of every month at twelve nodal villages drawn from six mandals of Anantapur district.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

81

It serves 63 target villages, besides patients who come from about 400 villages in the region.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

82

About 500 doctors from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, across twelve specialties, serve the Mobile Hospital as volunteers.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

83

The Mobile Hospital carries ultrasound with colour doppler, a 2D echocardiogram, digital x-ray and a laboratory.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

84

A college for women was built in Anantapur, the district capital, in 1968.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

85

In 1981 Baba set up the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning, a deemed university.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

86

The Sri Sathya Sai Higher Secondary School at Prasanthi Nilayam was started in 1983.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

87

On 23 June 1981 Baba founded the Sri Sathya Sai Gurukulam English Medium School at Rajamahendravaram.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

88

Baba launched the Sri Sathya Sai Vidya Vahini programme on 23 November 2010, for holistic education for all.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

89

After the Odisha floods of September 2008 left about four million people homeless across 6,000 villages, the Trust announced a project to rebuild their houses.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

90

Work began on 30 November 2008, and by the end of March 2009 two hundred houses were complete and ready to live in.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

91

By 5 November 2009 around 700 houses in 16 villages of Cuttack, Kendrapara and Puri had been built and handed over, in less than a year.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

92

A second phase from January 2013 built 300 more houses in eight villages.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

93

In all, 1,000 houses were built for 1,000 poor families, as Baba had promised, at an outlay of nearly 15 crore rupees.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

94

After the Bhuj earthquake of January 2001, Baba Himself flagged off a convoy of thirty trucks of relief material from Prasanthi Nilayam.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

95

After the tsunami of 26 December 2004, volunteers carried relief to more than 5,000 families in Nagapattinam district and Pondicherry.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

96

In 1978 Baba built the Jamia Masjid mosque; until then the Muslims of the area, mostly poor farmers, prayed in a hut that held about fifteen people.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

97

The Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust was founded by Sri Sathya Sai Baba on 2 September 1972.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

98

The Trust has commissioned a 6 MW solar power plant to run all of its institutions and ashrams.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

99

In January 2017 the Trust set up its first solar plant, of 100 kWp, at the Whitefield hospital.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

100

The hospital replaced its entire lighting with 3,000 LED tubes, cutting the lighting load by 60 per cent and saving up to 10,000 units of energy a month.

Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust

1 / 100

Every card links to the page it is published on. These come from Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust and Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences — the published accounts of the organisations that did the work. The figures are theirs and have not been independently audited.
