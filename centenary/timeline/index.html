One hundred years

52 moments. Swipe through them.

A boy in Puttaparthi

192623 November

Born

Sathyanarayana Raju is born on a Monday morning in the month of Kartika, in a thatched house where his father keeps a small shop in the next room.

A boy in Puttaparthi

1930s

The walk to school

From the age of eight he walks two and a half miles to school at Bukkapatnam, carrying his midday meal — and in the monsoon, wading neck-deep water with his school bag on his head.

A boy in Puttaparthi

1930s

The Pandhari bhajan group

He gathers sixteen or eighteen boys into a singing group and serves as its organiser, treasurer, composer and lead singer. Soon the neighbouring villages ask for them.

A boy in Puttaparthi

1930s

Kamalapuram

Sent to live with his elder brother, he wakes at four to the bell of a steam engine and carries two pots of water before school. He gives away his share of food to whoever has none.

A boy in Puttaparthi

194023 May

Sugar candy and flowers

He gives out sweets and flowers from empty hands. Asked by his father whether he is god, ghost or madcap, he answers: I am Sai Baba.

A boy in Puttaparthi

194020 October

“I am no longer your Sathya”

Standing in his brother’s doorway he sets down his schoolbooks for the last time, and walks out to a garden where a crowd gathers to sing the first bhajan he teaches them.

A place to come to

194514 December

The first mandir

On Vaikunta Ekadashi a tin shed with an annexe is opened on land given by two widows, Subbamma and Kamalamma. It is home for five years; the largest room is the prayer hall.

A place to come to

194725 May

The letter

At twenty-one he writes to his brother Seshama Raju setting out, in full, the mission he has come for.

A place to come to

194725 October

The first discourse

He speaks in public for the first time, at Karur in Tamil Nadu. Thousands of discourses follow over the next sixty-three years.

A place to come to

194814 January

A foundation is laid

Work begins on Prasanthi Nilayam, the Abode of Supreme Peace.

A place to come to

195023 November

Prasanthi Nilayam

The ashram is inaugurated on his twenty-fifth birthday. It remains the centre of everything that follows.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1954

A hospital for the villages

The foundation stone is laid for the Sri Sathya Sai General Hospital. When it opens in 1956 it is the first medical care of any kind in the region.

Teaching, and the first institutions

19571 July

The banyan

He plants a banyan on the hill behind the ashram for those who come to meditate. It is standing still.

Teaching, and the first institutions

19582 February

Sanathana Sarathi

The ashram begins a monthly magazine. Over the next quarter century he writes fifteen series of articles for it in plain spoken Telugu — Prema Vahini, Dharma Vahini, Dhyana Vahini and the rest.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1959June

The Meditation Tree

Beneath the banyan he installs a copper yantra, about fifteen inches by ten, materialised earlier that year from the sands of the Chitravathi.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1960

The Seva Organisations

He begins the Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations in India, so that people of every religion, class and caste can serve together. In time they reach more than 126 countries.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1960

Sathyam Sivam Sundaram

Professor N. Kasturi of Mysore University publishes the first volume of the biography.

Teaching, and the first institutions

19621 October

Veda Purusha Jnana Saptaha Yajna

The week-long Dasara yajna is inaugurated, and Vedic scholars come from across India to speak on Sanathana Dharma as a way of living.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1963Guru Poornima

The three Sais

In the discourse that day he speaks of the triune advent — Shirdi Sai, Sathya Sai, Prema Sai.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1964

Brindavan

A second ashram is established at Whitefield, on the outskirts of Bangalore.

Teaching, and the first institutions

196812 May

Dharmakshetra, and the first World Conference

The Bombay headquarters is opened, with devotees arriving from more than twenty countries across four continents.

Teaching, and the first institutions

196830 June

East Africa

He leaves India for the only time in his life.

Teaching, and the first institutions

196822 July

A college for women

The college opens at Anantapur — the first of the campuses that become the university.

Teaching, and the first institutions

19699 June

A college for men

The second college opens at Brindavan, Whitefield.

Teaching, and the first institutions

1972May

The first Summer Course

A summer school in Indian culture and spirituality is held for students from across the country, who also go out into the villages to serve.

Teaching, and the first institutions

19722 September

The Central Trust

The Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust is founded to carry the charitable work. Every project on this page after this date is its doing.

Teaching, and the first institutions

197217 October

Poornachandra Auditorium

The great hall at Prasanthi Nilayam is opened.

The mission widens

1975

Sarva Dharma Stupa

At the fiftieth birthday he inaugurates the pillar that stands for the unity of the world’s religions.

The mission widens

197628 August

The hospital at Whitefield

A general hospital opens on the outskirts of Bangalore. The same year, Srimathi Easwaramma High School opens at Puttaparthi.

The mission widens

198115 June

Primary and higher secondary schools

The schools at Prasanthi Nilayam open, completing a path from the first class to a degree.

The mission widens

198122 November

A university

The Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning is inaugurated in the presence of the Chief Justice of India.

The mission widens

1983

Books and Publications

A publishing trust is established to carry the teachings, and has done so ever since.

The mission widens

1985

The planetarium

A planetarium opens at Prasanthi Nilayam, so that students might look up.

The mission widens

1990

Hanuman on the hill

A statue sixty-five feet high is raised at the top of the Hill View Stadium for the sixty-fifth birthday.

The mission widens

199115 November

An airport

An airport is inaugurated at Prasanthi Nilayam.

The mission widens

199122 November

The super-speciality hospital

A 230-bed hospital opens at Prasanthigram, giving heart and kidney surgery at the highest level, free, to anyone who needs it. Its dome rises ninety feet.

Water

1995March

Anantapur begins

Work starts on bringing drinking water to a district whose rivers run dry and whose groundwater carried enough fluoride to deform bone.

Water

199518 November

The first water arrives

The Prime Minister inaugurates the first phase. In eighteen months some 2,000 kilometres of pipeline reach about 730 villages and 1.25 million people.

Water

19959 July

Sai Kulwant Hall

A hall is opened that holds twenty thousand people at a time for darshan.

Water

199730 December

The Unity Cup

An India XI captained by Sachin Tendulkar plays at the Hill View Stadium. Earlier that year, the first Sai World Youth Conference is held.

Water

199923 November

A postage stamp

The Department of Posts issues a stamp and a postal cover for the water work.

Water

200018 November

Chaitanya Jyothi

A museum of the life and message opens for the seventy-fifth year of the advent.

Water

200119 January

Whitefield’s hospital

A second free super-speciality hospital, of 333 beds, is opened by the Prime Minister — built in nine months and six million man hours.

Water

2001

Medak, and the radio

The water model is repeated for 320 villages in Medak and Mahbubnagar. Radio Sai begins broadcasting from Prasanthi Nilayam, around the clock.

Water

200219 January

Water for Chennai

He announces that Krishna water will reach a city of millions that was managing on a third of what it needed.

Water

200423 November

The gates open

On his seventy-ninth birthday the Kandaleru gates are raised. The water covers 150 kilometres in four days, and the Poondi reservoir begins to fill.

Water

200622 November

A stadium

The President of India opens an Olympic-standard indoor stadium, a hundred metres by sixty.

Water

200715 September

The Godavari districts

Water projects serving some 452 upland and tribal habitations are handed to the state. That year, 6,000 young people from 85 countries come to Prasanthi Nilayam.

What was left behind

20087 October

A thousand houses

After floods leave about four million people in Odisha homeless, a housing project is announced. In both phases, 1,000 houses are built for 1,000 families.

What was left behind

2010November

The eighty-fifth year

The advent is celebrated across India and in 125 countries. He gives his last discourse at the twenty-ninth convocation of the university.

What was left behind

201124 April

He leaves the body

After eighty-six years. The schools, the hospitals, the water and the organisations carry on.

What was left behind

202623 November

One hundred years

A century since the morning in the thatched house. You are reading this because somebody in North Dallas wanted you to mark it with them.
