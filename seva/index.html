Seva

Service as a spiritual practice

Service at the Sai Baba Center is primarily a spiritual practice, aimed at elevating us through constant practice to recognise the Divinity in all beings.

What is seva?

As Swami says, “The best way to please me is to see Me in all beings and serve them just as you would like to serve Me.”

Swami also teaches that before engaging in seva — selfless service — one must attain purity of heart. It is important to examine one’s motives, skills, intentions and qualifications, and to be aware of the desire for fame or egoism. To truly serve, one must relinquish pride, status, wealth, scholarship and position, and instead practise humility, obedience, discipline and compassion.

How can we help?

  • Help as much as you can.
  • Help as efficiently as you can.
  • Help as silently as you can.
  • Help as lovingly as you can.

Leave the rest to God, who gave you the chance to serve.

Why join a group service project?

Swami says, “What a single individual cannot accomplish, a well-knit group or society can achieve. A man walking alone will feel tired and miserable at the end of five miles, but walking with a group would find the five miles a happy jaunt. He arrives refreshed and strong.”

Our projects are designed to meet the diverse needs and abilities of participants. If none of these projects address your specific areas of interest, please contact the seva coordinator to find a suitable opportunity.

Get involved

Standing seva opportunities

These run on a fixed rhythm through the year. To join any of them, talk to your child’s teacher.

First Friday of every month

Narayan Seva

A home-cooked vegetarian meal served at the Austin Street shelter in Dallas.

More about this seva

First Saturday of every month, 10 am

Temple Cleaning Seva

The Shirdi Baba temple in Plano has been home to our unit presentations. Children are encouraged to take part as an expression of gratitude.

Every day

Breakfast Seva

A healthy, nutritious breakfast served to the homeless in Dallas, led by one of our own teachers.

Every other month

Sheets & Parking

Volunteers collect our white sheets and return them washed. Parking volunteers could use your help too.

Through the year

Special service projects

October

UNICEF Trick-or-Treat

Bal Vikas students trick-or-treat as UNICEF volunteers to support children’s causes worldwide.

November

Birthday Care Kits

Devotees assemble care kits for the homeless in Dallas to help them through the winter. Distributed by senior Bal Vikas students, they include handmade hats and scarves created by the children themselves.

November

Thanksgiving Meals-on-Wheels

Families deliver meals to the elderly, lonely and homebound on the holiday, in a project organised by the local Visiting Nurses Association.

Regular projects

Austin Street Center

A wholesome, home-cooked vegetarian meal served on the first Friday of every month.

Read more

Park Cleaning

Students clean parks and streams in Plano.

Feed My Starving Children

Children and their families take part in packing meals.

Nursing homes

Children visit elderly residents to play games, sing songs, make crafts and share their love. Holidays and birthdays are specially planned to make our elderly friends feel remembered.
